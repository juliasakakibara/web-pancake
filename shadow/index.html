Shadow

Elevation shadows. L1 shadow.1–4 define progressively deeper drop-shadows; L2 aliases (card, dropdown, modal, tooltip, focus) point at the right primitive for each surface role. The focus shadow (L1 shadow.ring) is available for inset focus indicators; no shipped component uses it — focus states in the system are drawn with outline instead.
